# Spaarke hero — creative brief

*For Claude Design · v1.0 · April 2026*

---

## Project

Spaarke is a legal operations intelligence platform that serves both corporate legal departments and the law firms they hire. It runs on Microsoft 365 (Dataverse, SharePoint, Azure AI Foundry) and consolidates matters, documents, spend, performance assessment, and AI into one shared system. Currently in early access.

## Strategic position

**The moat:** Spaarke is the only legal platform built for both sides of the engagement. Every other vendor sees one side — the firm's billing, or the department's spend, or the firm's documents, or the department's matter list. Spaarke sees both, which is what enables cross-client benchmarks and shared intelligence no single-sided tool can produce.

**The category claim:** *The shared system of record for legal.* Not "for" Microsoft 365 — *on* it. Spaarke is foundational. AI tools (Microsoft 365 Copilot, Harvey, Legora, custom agents) run on top of it.

**What we don't claim:** Not a Copilot accessory, not a contract review tool, not an e-billing platform, not "AI for legal." Those are crowded categories owned by other vendors.

## The hero

**Headline:** Both sides of every matter.

**Subhead:** The shared system of record for legal departments and the firms they hire.

**Primary CTA:** Get access *(high contrast, solid)*
**Secondary CTA:** Watch demo *(outline)*

**Trust strip below the product:** Built on Microsoft 365 · Copilot-native · Azure AI Foundry — OpenAI, Anthropic, and any frontier model

**Top banner above hero:** Now accepting early access partners — request access today

**Layout:**
- Centered headline and subhead with generous whitespace
- Two CTAs centered below the subhead
- Full-bleed Spaarke product screenshot below the text block
- Trust strip (one quiet line) directly below the screenshot
- Reference patterns: Linear, Stripe, Vercel, Notion — confident, restrained, dark-friendly

## Visual direction

**Aesthetic:** Sophisticated enterprise, *Bloomberg Businessweek* and *Monocle* references, not SaaS marketing. Dark mode is preferred — Spaarke's product UI is dark and the brand should feel cohesive.

**Typography:** Sans-serif, two weights only (regular and medium). Sentence case throughout — never Title Case, never ALL CAPS. Slight tight letterspacing on the headline (around -0.02em).

**Color:** Restrained. Single accent color permitted. No gradients, no glow effects, no neon, no noise textures, no decorative gradients behind the hero text.

**Spacing:** Generous. Whitespace is the primary visual element.

The Spaarke workspace screenshot is the hero visual. It shows the Corporate Counsel workspace with:
- Left sidebar (Work, Information, Communication, Activities, Network groups)
- Main dashboard (Get Started, Quick Summary, Latest Updates, My To Do List, Active Documents)
- Right Copilot panel with Spaarke AI agent

**Cleanup required before use:**

- Remove the "SANDBOX" badge in the top right
- Replace test data with realistic legal data. Examples:
  - Matter names: *Acme — Series C Lead Counsel*, *Project Lighthouse — IP Diligence*, *Henderson v. Cascade — Discovery Plan*
  - Document titles should sound like real legal work product
  - To-do items should sound like genuine practitioner tasks
- Remove the version footer (*"v1.0.2 — Built Apr 28, 2026"*)
- Lift contrast slightly for marketing readability (image-level adjustment, not a UI change)
- Keep the existing Copilot panel prompts — *"What are my overdue tasks?"*, *"Find documents for the Acme matter"*, *"Run a risk scan on this contract"* — they're exactly the right voice

## Brand vocabulary

These phrases should appear consistently across the entire site, not just the hero:

- *Both sides of every matter* — recurring tagline
- *The shared system of record* — category claim
- *For legal departments and the firms they hire* — audience naming
- *Fluent in legal* — AI capability claim, used in supporting sections
- *On Microsoft 365* — preposition matters; we're on the substrate, not for it

## Things to avoid

- AI-led headlines (*"AI for legal," "The AI workspace for lawyers"*) — Harvey, Legora, Hebbia own that ground
- Microsoft-led headlines (*"Legal-grade Microsoft 365"*) — Harvey took this in March 2026 with their Copilot integration
- Plugin / extension / add-on language anywhere — Spaarke is foundational, not subordinate
- Three-verb taglines (*"Work Smarter. Operate Leaner. Decide Faster."*) — generic SaaS
- Stock photography aesthetic — diverse-team-around-laptop, handshakes, pointing-at-screen
- AI-generated people in imagery — real brand-damage risk for a legal-AI company
- Title Case in headlines — sentence case throughout

## Buyer context (for Claude's reference)

The 2026 legal operations buyer is navigating a structural productivity gap — demand rising in regulatory and cybersecurity work while headcount and outside counsel spend are flat (CLOC 2026 State of the Industry Report). Tool fragmentation is the most visceral pain — 70% of in-house lawyers spend over an hour a day switching between systems. AI is now table stakes (92% use AI daily), with the conversation shifted from *if* to *how, safely.* The CLD-firm relationship is being explicitly redefined around shared data and partnership rather than transactional billing. Spaarke is positioned to be the platform that resolves all four of these forces at once.
