## Introduction

The Spaarke platform has been designed to provide companies, professional services firms, and operations teams the tools they need to manage documents, matters, and AI-driven analysis across the full range of business activities. Purpose built for document-intensive workflows, the platform combines secure document storage, intelligent analysis, and configurable workspaces that streamline the complexities typically experienced with traditional document management and matter management systems. Spaarke allows for easy access to the documents, records, and AI-powered insights needed for informed decision making.

Built on the Microsoft Power Platform and SharePoint Embedded, users work in a familiar software environment including direct integration to Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI, Microsoft 365, Outlook/Exchange, Teams, and SharePoint—plus Microsoft Azure enterprise-class architecture that ensures 24×7 access, performance, data integrity, and security protections. The platform is easily extended through Microsoft's Power Platform™ tools allowing for custom processes, analytics, and other capabilities.

The core modules include:

- Workspaces (Legal, Corporate, External)
- Document Management
- Document Intelligence
- AI Playbooks
- Conversational AI Assistant
- Matter and Project Management
- Smart Task Management
- Client and External Portal
- Self-Service Registration
- Microsoft 365 Integration
- Microsoft Teams Integration
- Microsoft Power BI for reporting and analytics
- Microsoft SharePoint Embedded for document storage
- Microsoft Azure cloud infrastructure

## Workspaces

The Workspaces module is the organizing concept at the heart of the Spaarke platform. Each workspace brings together the documents, records, conversations, tasks, and AI activities for a specific matter, project, or engagement, providing a single point of access for the team members who need to work together. Workspaces are role-aware, security-trimmed, and configured for the type of work being performed.

### Key features of Workspaces include:

#### Workspace Types

- Legal Operations Workspace tailored for matter management and legal document analysis
- Corporate Workspace for project, transaction, and general business document collaboration
- External Workspace for clients, counterparties, and outside contributors
- Configurable layouts allow each workspace type to surface the most relevant sections

#### Workspace Home

- Single landing page with documents, tasks, recent activity, and key updates
- Latest Updates feed with consolidated emails, comments, and system events
- To Do list with due-date tracking and priority badges
- Documents grid with quick filters, search, and inline preview
- Count badges show outstanding items at a glance

#### Workspace Configuration

- Workspace Layout Wizard lets administrators customize sections and ordering
- Themes and branding align workspace appearance with organizational identity
- Section visibility controlled by user role and permissions
- New workspace types can be defined without custom development

#### Membership and Access

- Internal team members granted role-based access (owner, contributor, reader)
- External members invited through the Client Portal with scoped permissions
- Permission inheritance from parent records (matter, project, account)
- Audit trail of access grants and removals

#### Drill-Through Navigation

- One-click access from workspace home to any related record
- Breadcrumb navigation across nested workspaces and records
- Universal Quick Create for adding records without leaving the workspace
- Embedded grids and forms eliminate context switching

## Document Management

The Document Management module supports the end-to-end lifecycle of business documents, from upload and classification through analysis, sharing, and archival. Built on Microsoft SharePoint Embedded, Spaarke provides secure, scalable storage with the rich metadata and relationship tracking required for legal, transactional, and compliance work.

### Key features of Document Management include:

#### Document Upload

- Multi-file Document Upload Wizard with drag-and-drop and folder support
- Required metadata captured at point of upload through configurable profiles
- Bulk upload with per-file metadata override
- Inbound email attachment ingestion through Outlook integration
- Automatic content extraction and full-text indexing on upload

#### Document Profiles

- Structured metadata profiles by document type (contract, invoice, filing, memo, etc.)
- AI-assisted metadata extraction populates fields automatically
- Profile fields drive search, filtering, and reporting across the platform
- Configurable profiles support new document types without custom development

#### Document Relationships

- Documents link to matters, projects, contacts, and other documents
- Document Relationship Viewer presents related documents as a navigable graph
- Related Document Count badges show linked items at a glance
- Versioning, supersession, and parent/child relationships tracked

#### Document Viewer

- Native preview of Office documents, PDFs, images, and email files
- Side-by-side viewing with AI analysis output and metadata
- Inline annotations and reading state preserved
- One-click open in Microsoft 365 desktop or web

#### Find Similar

- Semantic similarity search identifies documents with related content
- Hybrid search combines keyword, vector, and semantic ranking
- Find Similar Wizard supports both document lookup and ad-hoc file upload
- Results scoped by workspace, matter, or organization

#### Document Storage and Security

- All files stored in Microsoft SharePoint Embedded with encryption at rest and in transit
- Access controlled through Microsoft Entra ID with conditional access policies
- Document checkout and version history fully tracked
- Tenant isolation guarantees no cross-customer data exposure

## Document Intelligence

The Document Intelligence module provides AI-powered analysis, extraction, and classification across the document portfolio. Spaarke's AI Tool Framework offers a configurable library of analysis tools that operate on individual documents, document sets, or entire workspaces, with results streamed in real time and persisted for future reference.

### Key features of Document Intelligence include:

#### Streaming AI Analysis

- Token-by-token streaming output for immediate feedback during analysis
- Working document captures intermediate results and is preserved for review
- Long-running analyses complete in the background and notify on completion
- Cancel and re-run controls keep users in command of the process

#### Extraction and Classification

- Configurable extraction tools pull structured data from invoices, contracts, and filings
- Classification tools tag documents by type, topic, jurisdiction, and confidentiality
- Generic Analysis Handler powers most tools through configuration rather than code
- Extracted data flows into searchable fields and downstream workflows

#### Summarization

- Single-document and batch summarization through the Summarize Files Wizard
- Summary length, audience, and focus configurable per request
- Summaries saved to the document profile for future retrieval
- Multi-document summaries roll up findings across an entire workspace

#### Semantic Search

- Natural language queries return ranked document results across the corpus
- Hybrid retrieval combines BM25 keyword, vector embedding, and semantic ranking
- Security trimming ensures users only see results they are authorized to view
- Results include passage-level highlights and citations

#### AI Metadata Extractor

- Inline control extracts metadata directly into form fields
- Supports both single-field and full-profile extraction modes
- Confidence scores and source citations support human review
- Extraction templates configurable per document type

## AI Playbooks

The AI Playbooks module provides a visual, node-based environment for designing and executing multi-step AI workflows. Playbooks combine AI analysis, conditional logic, data updates, notifications, and outputs into reusable processes that codify how an organization handles specific document types, matters, or recurring activities.

### Key features of AI Playbooks include:

#### Visual Playbook Builder

- Drag-and-drop canvas for assembling playbook nodes and connections
- Node library includes AI analysis, conditions, data updates, email, tasks, and outputs
- Parameter and variable management at each step
- Versioning and publication workflow for playbook governance

#### Playbook Library

- Catalog of published playbooks discoverable by users
- Categories organize playbooks by department, document type, or use case
- Configurable visibility controls who can see and run each playbook
- Usage analytics track adoption and outcomes

#### AI Builder Agent

- Conversational interface to construct and modify playbooks through natural language
- Agentic loop interprets user intent and assembles canvas nodes automatically
- Generated playbooks reviewed and refined before publication
- Accelerates onboarding for non-technical playbook authors

#### Scope System (JSON Prompt Schema)

- Reusable Actions, Skills, Knowledge, and Tools standardize AI prompts
- Knowledge units provide retrieval-augmented context to playbooks
- Skills define instruction fragments that combine into Actions
- Tools encapsulate LLM operations with structured inputs and outputs

#### Execution and Scheduling

- Playbooks run on demand, on a schedule, or in response to events
- Background scheduler executes notification-driven runs at scale
- Execution history and logs preserved for audit and troubleshooting
- Failed runs route to a dead-letter queue for review and replay

## Conversational AI Assistant

The Conversational AI Assistant provides an in-workspace chat experience that brings AI capabilities directly into the user's daily workflow. The assistant operates with full awareness of the active workspace, document context, and available playbooks, allowing users to ask questions, run analysis, and take actions through natural language.

### Key features of the Conversational AI Assistant include:

#### In-Workspace Chat

- Persistent chat pane available throughout the workspace
- Multi-turn dialog with full conversation history
- Context-aware responses grounded in the active matter, project, or document
- Citations link back to source documents and records

#### Tool and Playbook Execution

- Assistant can invoke configured tools and playbooks during a conversation
- Structured outputs feed directly into records, tasks, or documents
- Confirmation prompts gate any action that modifies data
- Execution results presented inline and saved to the conversation transcript

#### Document Q&A

- Ask questions across one document, a set of documents, or the entire workspace
- Retrieval-augmented responses cite the specific passages used
- Follow-up questions build on prior context without re-uploading content
- Long documents handled through automated chunking and ranking

#### Personalization

- Assistant adapts tone and depth to user role and preferences
- Saved prompts and shortcuts speed common requests
- Cross-session memory preserves context for ongoing engagements

## Matter and Project Management

The Matter and Project Management module provides structured intake, tracking, and lifecycle management for legal matters, corporate projects, and other business engagements. Guided wizards capture the information required to launch a new engagement, and configurable record templates ensure consistency across the organization.

### Key features of Matter and Project Management include:

#### Create Matter Wizard

- Step-by-step creation of legal matters with required and optional metadata
- Client, parties, and contact linkage captured during creation
- Matter type drives field visibility and downstream workflow
- Initial document upload and folder structure configured at creation
- Created matter immediately available as a fully provisioned workspace

#### Create Project Wizard

- Equivalent guided creation flow for corporate projects and transactions
- Project type, business unit, and stakeholders captured up front
- Budget, timeline, and milestones set at creation
- Auto-association with related records (account, opportunity, deal)

#### Create Event and Create Todo

- Lightweight wizards for adding events, deadlines, and tasks to a workspace
- Priority, due date, and assignee captured in a single step
- Events appear in the Smart Task Kanban and calendar views
- Optional reminders and notifications

#### Create Work Assignment

- Standalone wizard for assigning work to internal or external counsel
- Captures scope, budget, deadline, and instructions
- Routes to the assignee through email and portal notification
- Tracks acceptance, status updates, and completion

#### Record Card Shell

- Reusable card layout for matter, project, and engagement summary views
- Headline status, owner, dates, and key counts visible at a glance
- One-click drill-through to the full record or workspace
- Consistent presentation across grids, dashboards, and search results

## SharePoint Embedded Document Storage

SharePoint Embedded provides the secure, scalable, enterprise-grade document storage layer beneath the Spaarke platform. Each workspace is backed by a dedicated SharePoint container with all the security, governance, and compliance capabilities of Microsoft 365, while remaining invisibly integrated into the Spaarke user experience.

- Documents stored in dedicated containers per workspace, account, or matter
- Encryption at rest and in transit, with customer-managed keys available
- Full text search and indexing managed by the Microsoft 365 substrate
- Conditional access, sensitivity labels, and data loss prevention policies enforced
- Geographic data residency aligned with Microsoft 365 commitments
- No additional infrastructure for customers to deploy or maintain

---

## Background Processing and Orchestration

Long-running and high-volume operations are handled by a background processing layer that ensures user-facing experiences stay responsive while still completing intensive work reliably. AI analysis, document indexing, email ingestion, and bulk operations all flow through a managed job queue with retry, monitoring, and dead-letter handling.

- Service Bus job queue handles asynchronous AI analysis, indexing, and ingestion
- Document analysis, RAG indexing, and email processing scale independently
- Failed jobs route to a dead-letter queue with replay and inspection tools
- Embedding migration and vector backfill maintain search index health
- Job-level monitoring and alerting integrated with Azure Monitor

## Getting Started

With the Spaarke platform, clients work in an efficient, easy-to-use environment that supports every aspect of their document and matter operations. All critical data, documents, and workflows are supported with world class technology that optimizes the accuracy, reliability, and efficiency of business processes.

All Spaarke clients are onboarded to the platform to gain the benefits of its capabilities. We start by importing the client's existing documents and records, either from their current systems or through provided spreadsheets and exports. The data goes through a structured review and verification process, ensuring metadata is consistent, relationships are preserved, and content is correctly indexed for search and AI analysis.

In addition to data onboarding, we configure the client's account including workspace types, document profiles, AI playbooks, and integration to Microsoft 365, Teams, and other enterprise systems. We work with the client to define reporting templates, dashboards, and notifications tailored to their organization.

Finally, we provide the client with both getting-started training and ongoing training to ensure the Spaarke platform delivers value and continues to support the client's evolving needs.
